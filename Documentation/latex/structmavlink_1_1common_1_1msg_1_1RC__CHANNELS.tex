\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\mbox{\hyperlink{structmavlink_1_1common_1_1msg_1_1RC__CHANNELS}{RC\+\_\+\+CHANNELS}} message. 

The PPM values of the RC channels received. The standard PPM modulation is as follows\+: 1000 microseconds\+: 0\%, 2000 microseconds\+: 100\%. A value of UINT16\+\_\+\+MAX implies the channel is unused. Individual receivers/transmitters might violate this specification.
