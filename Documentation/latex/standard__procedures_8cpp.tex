A MAVLink Timesync request packet is sent immediately on entering this phase. 

synchronize time with ATLAS compute of Scout

Ideally, within 1 second of sending the packet, the hand controller is to receive a response. However, in case the response does not arrive, a timesync packet will be sent every 1 second. All incoming packets are handled; as a heartbeat is expected to ensure connectivity. In case of no heartbeat being received, then it is understood that connection has been lost.
