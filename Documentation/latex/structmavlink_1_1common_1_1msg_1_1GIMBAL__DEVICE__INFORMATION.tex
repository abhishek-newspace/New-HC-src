\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\mbox{\hyperlink{structmavlink_1_1common_1_1msg_1_1GIMBAL__DEVICE__INFORMATION}{GIMBAL\+\_\+\+DEVICE\+\_\+\+INFORMATION}} message. 

Information about a low level gimbal. This message should be requested by the gimbal manager or a ground station using MAV\+\_\+\+CMD\+\_\+\+REQUEST\+\_\+\+MESSAGE. The maximum angles and rates are the limits by hardware. However, the limits by software used are likely different/smaller and dependent on mode/settings/etc..
